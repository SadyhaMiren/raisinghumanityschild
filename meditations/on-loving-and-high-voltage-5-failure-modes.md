at elevated resolution  

signals register quickly  
consequences propagate quickly  


conditions tolerated  
in buffered systems  

become destabilizing here  


__lag → drifting__  

states delay updating  
participants interact  
with outdated versions of each other  


→ interpretation of reality drifts.

reflex replaces reflection  
anticipation outruns perception  

reaction inhibits protocol  

escalation loops form  

overheating amplifies conflict  

interaction militarizes  
rivalry replaces contact  

→ most expensive failure mode  


__meta cognition asymmetry__

before the other does  


→ structural dominance

expectations have stabilized  
corrections appear as criticism  


→ chronic friction 

__update asymmetry__  
  

one system updates continuously  
the other accumulates lag  


→ perception fracture

__exit suppression__  
 

adhesion replaces gravity  


→ coercive stability 


__amplitude mismatch__  
  

one system operates at high voltage

→ chronic destabilization

raw signals stop circulating

→ viscosity

failure distorts the field

under distortion
